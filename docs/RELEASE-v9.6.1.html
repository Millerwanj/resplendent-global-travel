Release v9.6.1 — Complete Client Engagement Suite

Release date: 25 July 2026

v9.6.1 is a cumulative production release. It includes every v9.6.0 feature and adds the Invoice Generator and editable Payment Settings.

Invoice Generator

  • Creates invoices directly or converts an approved quotation.
  • Uses RGT-I-YYYY-000001 sequential numbering.
  • Supports Travel & Services, Corporate Travel and Business Matchmaking.
  • Business Matchmaking can invoice USD 250 on engagement, USD 150 mid-project, USD 100 on final delivery, or the complete USD 500 professional fee.
  • Calculates subtotal, discounts, service fees, total, amount paid and balance.
  • Tracks Issued, Part-paid, Paid and Overdue states.
  • Allows payment-position updates from the generated invoice.
  • Produces a branded print-ready document for browser PDF export.

Payment Settings

  • Private admin configuration for bank name, account name, account number, branch, SWIFT/IBAN, account currency, M-Pesa and a secure payment link.
  • Defaults to “Payment instructions will be provided separately.”
  • Can be changed without editing code or redeploying the website.
  • New invoices use the latest settings.
  • Existing invoices retain the payment details captured when they were issued.

Cumulative preserved features

  • Proposal and Quotation Generators.
  • Client Workflow with Invoice and Payment stages.
  • Enhanced Success Page after Begin Your Journey submission.
  • Zoho lead creation, departmental SMTP routing and customer acknowledgement.
  • Approved public design, SEO, analytics, navigation and content.
