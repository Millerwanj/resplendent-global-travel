# Internal pricing policy — v10.8

This policy is private and must not appear in client-facing pages or documents.

- Minimum base margin: 25%.
- Working allowance for payment processing and foreign-exchange movement: 3%.
- Minimum client total: `total supplier cost / 0.72`.
- The quotation generator requires a confirmed supplier-cost basis and blocks a
  travel or corporate quotation below the calculated floor.
- Discounts must not reduce the grand total below the floor.
- High-season, solo-traveller and exceptional supplier supplements are priced
  privately in the tailored proposal.
- Peak-season protection always begins with the actual seasonal supplier cost;
  the margin and reserve must not be used to conceal an outdated low-season
  cost basis.

Public starting prices are planning anchors only and do not replace a live cost
check for the requested dates.
