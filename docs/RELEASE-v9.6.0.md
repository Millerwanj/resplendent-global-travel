# Release v9.6.0 — Client Engagement Suite Production

**Release date:** 25 July 2026

## Production scope

v9.6.0 extends the verified v9.5.2 production baseline with four operational
features while preserving the approved public website design.

### Proposal Generator

- Private `/admin/` access.
- Luxury Travel, Corporate Travel and Global Business Connections proposal modes.
- Client details, internal requirements, travel recommendations, included services and investment summary.
- Two-sentence recommendation guard.
- Automatic 5% travel service fee, grand total and `RGT-YYYY-000001` numbering.
- Separate USD 500 business-connection mandate and 250 / 150 / 100 milestone structure.
- Live branded preview and print-ready document output.

### Quotation Generator

- Repeatable travel/corporate line items, quantities and unit values.
- Discounts, service fees and automatic totals.
- Separate Business Matchmaking quotation with fixed USD 250 / 150 / 100 milestones.
- `RGT-Q-YYYY-000001` numbering and print-ready document output.

### Client Workflow

- Future website enquiries are written automatically to a private operations store.
- Stages: New Enquiry, Discovery, Proposal, Quotation, Approval, Service, Relationship and Closed.
- Search, stage filtering, client details, progress notes and activity history.
- Generating a proposal or quotation advances the linked client record.

### Enhanced Success Page

- Dedicated, branded confirmation page after a successful enquiry.
- Displays the enquiry reference and explains the three immediate next steps.
- Confirms one-business-day response expectation and provides support links.

## Preserved production systems

- Zoho CRM Web-to-Lead capture.
- Departmental SMTP delivery and central copy.
- Customer acknowledgement.
- Protected pipeline logging.
- Google Analytics ID `G-5WEEFVG6MB`.
- SEO metadata, canonical URLs, public navigation, copy, imagery and visual design.
