Resplendent Global Travel Solutions v10.8.1

Virungas Pricing Revision

Deployment candidate prepared: 10 August 2026
Deployment target: Verpex public_html

Public scope

  • Revises the Across the Virungas starting price to USD 14,100 per person sharing.
  • Updates the visible Journey Investment amount and matching TouristTrip Offer structured data together.
  • Retains the Masai Mara starting price of USD 7,350 and Watamu starting price of USD 2,400 per person sharing.
  • Keeps international flights quoted separately unless expressly included in a client proposal.

Design lock

No stylesheet, typography, colour, spacing, navigation, hero, image, responsive rule or public form style is changed. The approved v10.8 design and enquiry flow remain intact.

Commercial and payment controls

  • The internal quotation policy continues to protect a 25% base margin plus a separate 3% payment and foreign-exchange reserve.
  • RTGS bank transfer remains the preferred live payment method.
  • The provider-neutral foundation remains ready for a future approved online, mobile or contactless provider without activating one in this release.
