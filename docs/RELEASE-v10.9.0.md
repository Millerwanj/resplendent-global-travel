### Serengeti price basis

The confirmed 2026 partner nett rate was used for six green-season nights on
the Game Package. Compulsory Serengeti park entrance and camping fees were added
for every night. The resulting supplier cost was divided by 0.72 to protect the
approved 25% base margin and separate 3% payment/FX reserve, then rounded to the
public USD 6,800 starting price.

The public price includes full-board accommodation, selected drinks, laundry,
twice-daily scheduled shared game drives, one two-hour guided walking safari,
designated-airstrip transfers and compulsory park/camping fees. Flights,
premium drinks, gratuities, evacuation cover and optional ballooning remain
separate unless expressly included in a confirmed proposal.

No confidential partner rate sheet or trade-pack archive is included in the
deployment package.
