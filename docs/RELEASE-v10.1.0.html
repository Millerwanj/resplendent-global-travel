v10.1.0 — Across the Virungas Edition

Prepared 4 August 2026 from the v10.0.0 Signature Journeys Edition production baseline.

Release scope

  • Homepage: Across the Virungas becomes the current Featured Journey with a mountain gorilla photographed in Uganda’s Bwindi forest.
  • Signature Journeys: the Masai Mara remains in the collection and a second Rwanda–Uganda journey is added.
  • Across the Virungas: an eight-day, seven-night private framework connecting Kigali, Mgahinga Gorilla National Park and Volcanoes National Park.
  • Journal: a new original guide covering route logic, trekking preparation, conservation rules, permits, pacing and cultural context.
  • Enquiry: every Tailor This Journey route preselects Leisure Travel and prefills the journey name and Rwanda–Uganda destination.
  • Search: canonical metadata, TouristTrip, Article, CollectionPage, breadcrumbs and two new sitemap entries.

Media position

The release uses seven Rwanda and Uganda photographs across eight placements, available under the Unsplash License. The adult-and-infant gorilla photograph intentionally links the collection card with the journey hero through two different crops; every other placement remains unique. The set adds a photographic hero to the Signature Journeys collection and supplies independent imagery for the homepage feature, journey and Journal.

The Unsplash License does not require visible attribution, so no source caption interrupts the public design. Photographer, source, licence, transformation and output-dimension records are retained in docs/IMAGE-CREDITS.md. No Volcanoes Safaris image or film is embedded or bundled.

Release status

Prepared as a supplier-review candidate for local VS Code review. Do not deploy or push until the visual review is accepted and Volcanoes Safaris has given written approval of the completed feature. All published supplier names, permits, room availability, rates and operating terms remain subject to live confirmation when a client enquires.
