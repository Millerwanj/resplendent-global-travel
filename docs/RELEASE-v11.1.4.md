# Release v11.1.4 — Distinct Watamu and Masai Mara Explore Pages

## What changed

- The Signature Journeys hub now routes the Masai Mara card directly to `signature-journey-masai-mara`.
- The Signature Journeys hub now routes the Watamu card directly to `signature-journey-watamu`.
- Watamu now has a journey-specific enquiry form focused on coastal interests.
- Diani is presented as an optional South Coast alternative without diluting the featured Watamu itinerary.
- Masai Mara now has a separate journey-specific enquiry form focused on safari interests.
- On both journey pages, the starting price appears immediately before the enquiry form.
- Watamu's visible journey content remains coast-specific; the broad Kenya landing page remains available for country-level organic search.
- Journey calls to action now lead to the relevant on-page enquiry form.
- Shared lead-form colour variables and page-width handling were reviewed for consistent desktop and mobile presentation.
