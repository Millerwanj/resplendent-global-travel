Release v9.6.2 — Complete Client Pipeline

Release date: 26 July 2026

v9.6.2 completes the operational journey from public enquiry through document delivery and verified client acceptance.

Public enquiry experience

  • The separate thank-you page has been removed.
  • A successful submission returns to the Contact form.
  • The button changes to Submitted.
  • A restrained confirmation below the form displays the enquiry reference.
  • The existing Zoho Lead, departmental email, central copy and customer acknowledgement continue unchanged.

Document delivery

  • Proposal, quotation and invoice PDFs are generated on the server.
  • The approved Resplendent logo is embedded in every PDF.
  • The administrator must confirm a final review before sending.
  • The cover subject, client email and concise cover message remain editable.
  • The PDF is attached automatically through authenticated SMTP.
  • Each successful or failed delivery is recorded against the document and client workflow.

Client acceptance

  • Proposals and quotations can include a secure one-time acceptance link.
  • New sends supersede earlier outstanding acceptance links for the same document.
  • Acceptance requires the receiving email, full name, confirmation of terms and confirmation of authority.
  • The accepted PDF hash, policy version, UTC time and privacy-preserving connection fingerprint are recorded.
  • Successful acceptance moves the client to Approval and sends one internal notification. It does not send another client email.

Approved commercial wording

  • Travel documents include the approved supplier availability and pricing wording.
  • Business matchmaking documents include the approved milestone non-refundability wording, legal-rights safeguard and no-guaranteed-outcome wording.
  • The same policy snapshot appears in the preview, final HTML, attached PDF and acceptance page.

Preserved

  • Proposal, Quotation and Invoice Generators.
  • USD 250 / USD 150 / USD 100 business matchmaking schedule.
  • Quotation-to-invoice conversion.
  • Editable private Payment Settings and invoice payment tracking.
  • Existing admin access, Zoho integration, SMTP configuration, public design, analytics, SEO, navigation and content.
