The Journey

One country.
An extraordinary encounter.

Come for the gorillas, but give the Virungas enough time to reveal the landscape around them.

This compact Rwanda journey begins in Kigali before moving north through the land of a thousand hills to Virunga Lodge. Mountain gorillas remain the defining encounter, balanced with golden monkeys, conservation context and the warmth of an all-inclusive lodge stay.

A suggested rhythm

Five days, carefully sequenced.

Based on the 4-night Virunga Gorilla Safari operated by Volcanoes Safaris and personalised by Resplendent around your dates, flights and preferences.

  1. Day 01

    Arrive in Kigali

    Meet your expert safari guide at Kigali International Airport and ease into Rwanda with an afternoon city experience. Overnight in Kigali at The Retreat with breakfast.

  2. Day 02

    Kigali to the Virungas

    Visit the Kigali Genocide Memorial before driving north through Rwanda's hills to Virunga Lodge. Before dinner, join a conservation fireside chat with Gorilla Doctors or the Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund.

  3. Day 03

    Mountain gorillas

    Track mountain gorillas in Volcanoes National Park, then visit the Ellen DeGeneres Campus of the Dian Fossey Gorilla Fund. Return to Virunga Lodge for time to recover, with complimentary spa treatments available.

  4. Day 04

    Golden monkeys

    Return to the bamboo forest to track Rwanda's rare golden monkeys. The afternoon is deliberately unhurried, with the lodge's community, conservation and wellness experiences available.

  5. Day 05

    Return to Kigali

    Drive back to Kigali for your onward flight. International departures should be planned for the evening to protect the journey from unnecessary haste.
