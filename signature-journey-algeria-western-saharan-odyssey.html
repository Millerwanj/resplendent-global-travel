The Journey

Feel the landscape change.

This is Algeria as a gradual passage: Mediterranean Algiers, Oran and Tlemcen before the Atlas gives way to oasis country and the Sahara.

The route is intentionally progressive. Guided heritage visits, workshops, palm groves, rock engravings and a red-desert 4x4 excursion create a journey with context, not simply a list of stops.

A suggested rhythm

Twelve days, west to desert.

The sequence follows the current private programme and will be reconfirmed around domestic schedules, availability, conditions and your preferred pace.

  1. Day 01

    Arrive gently in Algiers

    Airport welcome, private transfer and a first look at the centre as your arrival time allows.

  2. Day 02

    Read the capital through its landmarks

    Explore the Casbah with a guide, then continue to Notre-Dame d'Afrique, central Algiers and a museum visit according to the programme.

  3. Day 03

    Turn west toward Oran

    Travel by domestic flight or road, according to the confirmed arrangement, before an introductory discovery of Oran.

  4. Day 04

    From Oran to Tlemcen

    Continue exploring Oran before travelling inland to Tlemcen toward the end of the day.

  5. Day 05

    Enter Tlemcen's Andalusian story

    Trace the city's palaces, mosques, gardens and historic lanes with considered local interpretation.

  6. Day 06

    Reach the Saharan Atlas

    Travel south to Aïn Sefra, where the character of the landscape begins to shift at the foot of the Atlas.

  7. Day 07

    Arrive among Taghit's palms

    Continue to Taghit and explore its oasis, ksar and palm groves beneath the first great dunes of the journey.

  8. Day 08

    Dunes, engravings and sunset

    Spend time among dunes and palms, encounter ancient rock engravings and watch the light change before continuing to Béni Abbès.

  9. Day 09

    Discover Béni Abbès

    Explore the oasis, ksar and local heritage, including the chapel associated with Charles de Foucauld.

  10. Day 10

    Cross into the red desert

    Travel onward to Timimoun for a first immersion in the ochre landscapes that close the Saharan crossing.

  11. Day 11

    Explore by 4x4, then return north

    Venture into Timimoun's red desert by 4x4 before the included domestic flight to Algiers and a final overnight stay.

  12. Day 12

    Depart Algiers

    Private transfer to the airport and the close of the journey.
