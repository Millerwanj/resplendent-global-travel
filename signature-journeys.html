Tanzania · 7 days / 6 nights

The Resplendent Serengeti Signature Safari

Six nights in the south-central Serengeti, giving the Moru Kopjes and open plains time to unfold.

  1. Day 01
    Arrive above the plains

    Meet the camp team at Seronera and settle in before a first shared game drive, timing permitting.

  2. Day 02
    Find the Serengeti rhythm

    Early and late game drives with the middle of the day open for rest and the camp's long views.

  3. Day 03
    Walk with a professional guide

    A guided walk brings tracks, plants and the smaller details of the ecosystem into focus.

  4. Day 04
    Explore the Moru Kopjes

    Follow the day's wildlife through the south-central Serengeti and return for a sundowner.

  5. Day 05
    Give the plains a full day

    Let wildlife movement shape a longer exploration, with a picnic option when it suits the route.

  6. Day 06
    One final safari day

    Revisit a favourite landscape or keep the day flexible for new sightings.

  7. Day 07
    Depart, or continue Tanzania

    Transfer to Seronera for the onward flight or connect with another Tanzanian chapter.

Wildlife movement is natural and cannot be guaranteed. Activities remain subject to availability, conditions and park regulations.
