Kenya · 7 days / 6 nights

The Resplendent Maasai Mara Signature Safari

A private safari that gives the Mara time to unfold rather than turning wildlife into a checklist.

  1. Day 01
    Arrive gently in Nairobi

    Personal airport welcome, private transfer and time to rest before the safari begins.

  2. Day 02
    Fly into the Mara

    Air connection to the reserve, meet your safari team and ease into a first afternoon game drive.

  3. Day 03
    Find your safari rhythm

    Early and late game viewing with an unhurried midday pause back at camp.

  4. Day 04
    See the Mara differently

    A private full-day exploration, conservancy experience or optional sunrise balloon where conditions allow.

  5. Day 05
    Allow space to slow down

    Photography, a quiet breakfast, guided walking where permitted, or simply time to enjoy camp.

  6. Day 06
    One final full day

    Return to a favourite landscape or follow the wildlife movement with your guide.

  7. Day 07
    Depart, or continue the story

    Fly back to Nairobi or connect onward to the coast, another East African chapter or a city stay.

Kenya · Indian Ocean · 6 days / 5 nights

Watamu, by Tide & Dhow

Sea, stillness and Swahili-coast character, with enough open time for the coast to feel like a coast.

  1. Day 01
    Arrive by the Indian Ocean

    Private arrival transfer, a gentle check-in and an unhurried first evening by the water.

  2. Day 02
    Follow the water

    A dhow or marine experience shaped around tides, weather and the conditions of the day.

  3. Day 03
    Coast at your pace

    Leave room for the beach, pool, wellness and the luxury of having nowhere else to be.

  4. Day 04
    Add Swahili-coast character

    A considered cultural, culinary or local experience brings another layer to the shoreline.

  5. Day 05
    One last full horizon

    Revisit what you loved, keep the day deliberately light and close with the coast at sunset.

  6. Day 06
    Depart gently

    Private transfer for your onward flight, or let us connect Watamu to the next chapter.

Rwanda & Uganda · 8 days / 7 nights

Across the Virungas

Two mountain-gorilla landscapes, rare primates and volcanic highlands — planned with the preparation and breathing room these forests deserve.

  1. Day 01
    Arrive in Kigali

    Private welcome, transfer and a calm first night with time for a journey briefing.

  2. Day 02
    Into Rwanda's highlands

    Travel toward Volcanoes National Park, settle into the landscape and prepare for the forest.

  3. Day 03
    Mountain gorilla trek

    Enter the forest with the specialist park team for the first gorilla encounter, subject to permits and trekking conditions.

  4. Day 04
    Golden monkeys & conservation context

    A slower second forest day can bring golden monkeys, conservation insight or living culture into the story.

  5. Day 05
    Cross into Uganda

    A privately coordinated transfer carries the journey into Uganda's southwestern highlands.

  6. Day 06
    A second gorilla landscape

    Trek in Uganda for a different forest perspective and another deeply considered encounter.

  7. Day 07
    Let the forest breathe

    Choose a guided nature or community experience, conservation context, or simply a restorative final day.

  8. Day 08
    Return & depart

    Private onward coordination for the airport, Kigali or the next part of your journey.

Gorilla and primate activities depend on permit availability, park regulations, weather and traveller suitability.
