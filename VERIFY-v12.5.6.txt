RESPLENDENT FULL RELEASE v12.5.6
================================

Purpose
-------
Permanent eSIM catalogue resilience fix built from the complete v12.5.5 release.

Catalogue behaviour
-------------------
- 214 destination snapshots and 5,971 current offers ship with the release.
- A valid server snapshot is returned immediately instead of making the customer wait on the supplier API.
- The browser keeps a bounded 24-hour last-known-good copy for repeat visits.
- Supplier pricing refreshes in the background and is throttled to one attempt per 15 minutes.
- Failed or suspicious supplier responses never replace a valid snapshot.
- Checkout forces a current supplier-price refresh before an order and Paystack checkout are created.

Preserved
---------
- Complete Paystack journey, callback, webhook and reconciliation flow.
- Invoice, payments, receipt and branded eSIM delivery work from v12.5.4/v12.5.5.
- All pages, journals, images, international pages, SEO and existing site functionality.

Deployment
----------
Upload the complete archive contents into public_html and overwrite matching files.
Keep the private eSIMCard and payment configuration files outside public_html unchanged.
