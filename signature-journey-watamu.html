Tailor This Journey

Let the coast
set the pace.

Watamu works best when the itinerary leaves room for the tide. This journey is deliberately light on rigid scheduling: sea mornings, a considered dhow experience, time to slow down, and carefully chosen moments along the coast.

Resplendent shapes the stay around your preferred pace, room style, privacy, transfers and the experiences available for your dates.

A suggested rhythm

Five nights.
Plenty of horizon.

A starting framework rather than a fixed package.

Day 01

Arrive by the Indian Ocean

Private arrival coordination, a gentle check-in and an unhurried first evening by the water.

Day 02

Follow the water

A dhow or marine experience shaped around tides, weather and the conditions of the day.

Day 03

Coast at your pace

Leave room for the beach, pool, wellness and the luxury of having nowhere else to be.

Day 04

Add Swahili-coast character

A considered cultural, culinary or local experience brings another layer to the shoreline.

Day 05

One last full horizon

Revisit what you loved, keep the day deliberately light and close with the coast at sunset.

Day 06

Depart gently

Private transfer for your onward flight, or let us connect Watamu to another Kenya chapter.
