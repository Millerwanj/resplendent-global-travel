The Journey

A safari with room to breathe.

The most memorable Mara journeys are not built by filling every hour. They are built by placing each day well.

This seven-day framework combines a calm arrival, seamless access to the reserve or a private conservancy, thoughtfully paced game viewing and time to enjoy camp as part of the experience. Every final itinerary is tailored to the travellers, season and most suitable confirmed partners.

A suggested rhythm

Seven days, considered.

The sequence below is an editable framework. We will refine it around flight connections, ages, mobility, interests, occasion and preferred level of privacy.

  1. Day 01

    Arrive gently in Nairobi

    A personal airport welcome and private transfer create an unhurried first evening. Depending on arrival time, rest in Nairobi before the safari begins.

  2. Day 02

    Fly into the Mara

    Travel by scheduled or private air connection, meet your safari team and settle into the landscape with a first afternoon drive.

  3. Day 03

    Find your safari rhythm

    Early and late game viewing make use of the most rewarding hours, with the middle of the day left open for rest, reading or simply watching the plains.

  4. Day 04

    See the Mara differently

    Choose a private full-day exploration, conservancy experience or optional sunrise balloon flight, subject to weather, suitability and operator confirmation.

  5. Day 05

    Allow space to slow down

    A flexible day balances wildlife time with camp life. Photography, a quiet breakfast, a guided walk where permitted or time at leisure can shape the pace.

  6. Day 06

    One final full day

    Return to the places and rhythms that have mattered most, or let current wildlife movement guide a completely new direction.

  7. Day 07

    Depart, or continue the story

    Fly back to Nairobi for your onward journey, or extend to the Kenyan coast, another East African landscape or a private city stay.
