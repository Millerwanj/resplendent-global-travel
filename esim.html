An eSIM is a digital mobile plan installed on a compatible phone without replacing your physical SIM. It can provide travel data while your usual number remains available for calls, messages and verification where your device supports dual SIM.

Resplendent helps you consider destination coverage, data allowance, validity, hotspot support and activation requirements before you travel.

Install before departure

Do not wait until you land.

Connect to Wi-Fi, install the eSIM and follow the provider’s activation instructions before your flight. When you arrive, switch on the travel data line as directed.

Installation and activation steps vary by provider, destination and device. We will share the applicable instructions with your confirmed eSIM.

Estimate Your Data

Simple setup

How to install your eSIM.

Install while you still have reliable Wi-Fi. Exact menu names vary by device and provider, but the flow is usually straightforward.

01

Choose your plan

Select the destination, validity period and data allowance that match your trip.

02

Install the eSIM

Open the installation link or scan the QR code supplied with your plan while you have reliable internet access.

03

Select mobile data

In your phone settings, choose the travel eSIM as the line to use for mobile data abroad.

04

Connect on arrival

Follow the plan instructions when you land. Some plans may require data roaming to be enabled on the eSIM line.

Install before departure, but check when your specific plan begins its validity period. Keep your usual SIM active only as needed and avoid enabling international data roaming on the wrong line.

A practical starting point

How much data might you need?

Your actual use depends on app settings, video quality, background updates and whether you have regular Wi-Fi access.

01

Light use

1–3 GB per week

Messaging, email, maps and occasional browsing, with Wi-Fi available at hotels.

02

Everyday travel

5–10 GB per week

Regular navigation, social media, browsing, messaging and occasional calls over data.

03

Heavy use

15 GB+ per week

Remote work, frequent video calls, streaming, hotspot use or regular media uploads.

Before purchase

Four things to confirm.

Device compatibility

Your phone must support eSIM and should be network-unlocked.

Destination coverage

Country and regional plans differ by network availability and supported destinations.

Validity and activation

Some plans begin on installation; others begin when the eSIM first connects abroad.

Your normal SIM

Keep your usual line active only as required and manage roaming settings to avoid unintended charges.
