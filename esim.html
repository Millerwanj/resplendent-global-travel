Before you choose · 01

Check your phone

Is your phone eSIM ready?

Open your phone’s dial pad and enter *#06#. Look for an EID number in the information shown.

Enter *#06#

Use the Phone app on the device you will take on your trip.

Look for EID

An EID indicates eSIM hardware. An IMEI alone does not confirm eSIM support.

Confirm it is unlocked

Your phone must also accept another provider’s eSIM. If no EID appears, check Settings for “Add eSIM” or confirm your exact model with the manufacturer before buying.
