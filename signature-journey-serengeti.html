The Journey

Let the Serengeti unfold.

The Serengeti rewards time: first light on the plains, quiet hours at camp and the freedom to follow the day's wildlife rather than a checklist.

This seven-day framework is shaped around a premium tented camp in the Moru Kopjes area. It combines twice-daily scheduled shared game drives, a guided walk and designated-airstrip transfers with full-board accommodation and a considered selection of drinks.

A suggested rhythm

Seven days, considered.

The sequence below is an editable framework. We will refine it around flight connections, ages, mobility, interests, occasion and preferred level of privacy.

  1. Day 01

    Arrive above the plains

    Meet the safari team at Seronera airstrip, transfer to camp and settle in before a first scheduled shared game drive, timing permitting.

  2. Day 02

    Find the Serengeti rhythm

    Early and late game drives use the most rewarding hours, with the middle of the day open for lunch, rest and the camp's far-reaching views.

  3. Day 03

    Walk with a professional guide

    A two-hour guided walking safari adds a closer reading of tracks, plants and smaller details, subject to conditions, availability and traveller suitability.

  4. Day 04

    Explore the Moru Kopjes

    Follow the day's wildlife through the south-central Serengeti and return for a sundowner overlooking the plains.

  5. Day 05

    Give the plains a full day

    Let current wildlife movement shape the route, with a picnic option available when a longer exploration makes sense.

  6. Day 06

    One final safari day

    Revisit a favourite landscape or keep the day flexible for new sightings, leaving space to enjoy camp between drives.

  7. Day 07

    Depart, or continue Tanzania

    Transfer to Seronera airstrip for the onward flight, or connect the journey with Ngorongoro, Tarangire, Arusha or Zanzibar.
