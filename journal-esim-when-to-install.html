When Should You Install an eSIM — Before You Fly or After You Land?

For most travellers, the smoothest approach is to install the eSIM before departure, then activate or begin using it according to the provider’s instructions.

Why install before you leave?

After a long flight, the arrivals hall is not the ideal place to begin troubleshooting connectivity. Installing an eSIM while you still have reliable home Wi-Fi gives you time to confirm that the profile has been added correctly and to understand how to select it for mobile data.

Once you arrive, getting connected can then be as simple as selecting the travel eSIM and following the provider’s data settings.

Does installing it start the plan?

Not always. Installation and activation are not necessarily the same thing. Some travel plans begin when the eSIM first connects to a supported network at the destination; others use different validity rules.

Before installing, check exactly when the plan’s validity period begins and follow the instructions supplied with that particular eSIM.

What happens when you land?

Turn off airplane mode and follow the activation instructions provided with the eSIM. Typically, this means selecting the eSIM for mobile data and, where required, enabling data roaming on that line.

The aim is deliberately uneventful: arrive, switch on your phone and connect to a supported local network.

Can you keep your normal SIM?

On many modern dual-SIM smartphones, yes. You can often keep your normal line available for calls, SMS or services tied to your usual number while using the travel eSIM for mobile data.

Check your settings carefully, however. Leaving international data roaming enabled on the wrong line can lead to charges from your home mobile operator.

What about WhatsApp?

Using an eSIM for mobile data does not normally change the telephone number registered to your WhatsApp account. Your familiar WhatsApp account can generally continue to work while the eSIM supplies the internet connection.

Before you travel

Check that your phone supports eSIM technology and is network-unlocked. Choose a plan suitable for your destination, read its activation rules and install it while you have dependable internet access.

A few minutes of preparation before departure can make connectivity one less thing to solve after landing.
